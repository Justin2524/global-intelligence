# Global Intelligence design
White reading canvas, dark olive (OKLCH .30 .071 120) emphasis, neutral rules and pale olive Demo markers. Restrained palette. Georgia masthead and long-form headings; system sans-serif / PingFang SC for bilingual navigation and daily reading. No external fonts or tracking.
Desktop home uses an asymmetric daily lead and weekly feature, topic links and date-led article rows. Daily reports use numbered separators; weekly uses a dark introduction and continuous long-form typesetting. Mobile stacks columns, scrolls navigation horizontally, folds the article outline, uses 16px inputs and 44px primary touch targets. Reader zoom is never disabled. Native MathML renders formulas locally. Text and focus contrast, reduced motion and print output are supported.
