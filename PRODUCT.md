# Global Intelligence
## Register
brand — bilingual editorial knowledge library.
## Platform
web — mobile first, iPhone Safari.
## Users and purpose
Owner reads and submits complete reports from an iPhone; public readers browse global finance, technology and geopolitics, then follow original sources.
## Direction
Quiet, precise, evidence led. A reader on a daylight commute wants a ten-item digest before reading a longer weekly analysis. White canvas, dark olive ink accents, clear rules, generous line height. No live market figures or invented news. All seed content is Demo.
## Success
Three-field Issue Form → owner validation → immutable Markdown → same-workflow Pages deploy → verified URL and success comment. Accessible navigation, bilingual full-text search, native formula rendering.
